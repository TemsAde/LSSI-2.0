ABOUT US

Logistic Solution Service International Limited (RC NO: 1288113) was established to meet the rising demand for efficient and reliable supply chain solutions. We specialize in cold chain logistics, general commodity logistics, and comprehensive end-to-end logistics services managing the full journey of goods from origin to final delivery.

Our unique blend of advanced technology and experienced professionals allows us to deliver seamless, high-quality logistics solutions that meet international standards. Whether it's temperature-sensitive products or general cargo, we ensure safe, timely, and cost-effective movement across every stage of the supply chain.

At Logistic Solution Service International Limited, we are committed to exceeding client expectations by delivering precision, transparency, and excellence in every shipment.

CERTIFIED BY THE NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TRANSPORT TECHNOLOGY KADUNA (NIIT)
